Professor Yidu Yang – 79th Birthday Tribute 杨一都教授79岁生日纪念

扎根黔中，执着一事 Rooted in Guizhou, Devoted to One Pursuit

ResearchGate主页： profile: https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Yidu-Yang-2

杨一都教授，贵州师范大学博士生导师，享受国务院政府特殊津贴专家。1947年9月生，1977年毕业于贵州大学，1981年研究生毕业于武汉大学，获理学硕士学位，长期在贵州从事计算数学的教学与科研工作。

教学方面，杨一都教授始终将教书育人视为根本。作为第一完成人，他出版教材3本、学术专著1部，1998 年被评为贵州省优秀教师，1999 年获曾宪梓基金会高师优秀教师奖二等奖，2006年获贵州省五一劳动奖章，2012年获贵州省第八届高等教育省级教学成果奖贰等奖，同年被评为第三届贵州高校感动校园十大人物。迄今已指导毕业硕士研究生43名，毕业博士研究生9名，培养了一批活跃在大中学校教学和科研一线的骨干力量。

科研方面，他专注于偏微分方程特征值问题的数值方法研究，带领团队持续攻关。先后主持完成国家自然科学基金课题4项、贵州省科学研究课题6项。作为项目第一完成人，获贵州省科技进步奖贰等奖2项（1992、2015）、叁等奖3项（1991、1994、1997）。2015年被评为贵州省优秀科技工作者。

杨一都教授扎根贵州，培养与领导一个研究群体，专攻特征值问题有限元法，其研究成果不仅具有创新性，而且具有较重要的理论意义和工程实用价值，得到了中国科学院林群院士等同行专家学者的好评。

Professor Yidu Yang, a doctoral supervisor at Guizhou Normal University and a recipient of the Special Government Allowance granted by the State Council of China, was born in September 1947. He graduated from Guizhou University in 1977 and completed his postgraduate studies at Wuhan University in 1981, earning a Master of Science degree. He has long been engaged in teaching and research in computational mathematics in Guizhou.

In teaching, Professor Yidu Yang has always regarded teaching and nurturing students as his fundamental responsibility. As the lead author, he has published three textbooks and one academic monograph. In 1998, he was named an Outstanding Teacher of Guizhou Province; in 1999, he received the Second Prize of the Zeng Xianzi Foundation Award for Outstanding Teachers at Higher Normal Universities; in 2006, he was awarded the Guizhou Provincial May 1 Labor Medal; and in 2012, he received the Second Prize of the Eighth Guizhou Provincial Higher Education Teaching Achievement Award. In the same year, he was named one of the ten most inspirational campus figures at Guizhou universities in the third provincial selection. To date, he has supervised 43 master’s students and 9 doctoral students to completion, cultivating a group of key professionals active in teaching and research at universities and secondary schools.

In research, he has focused on numerical methods for eigenvalue problems of partial differential equations and has led his team in sustained research efforts. He has led and completed four projects funded by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and six scientific research projects funded by Guizhou Province. As the lead contributor, he received two Second Prizes (1992 and 2015) and three Third Prizes (1991, 1994, and 1997) of the Guizhou Provincial Science and Technology Progress Award. In 2015, he was named an Outstanding Science and Technology Worker of Guizhou Province.

Based in Guizhou, Professor Yidu Yang has cultivated and led a research group specializing in finite element methods for eigenvalue problems. His research achievements are not only innovative but also possess theoretical importance and practical engineering value. They have been well regarded by experts and scholars in the field, including Academician Qun Lin of the Chinese Academy of Sciences.
